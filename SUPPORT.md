# Support for SmileCat

## Report a problem or send feedback

Email contact@firstpoint.com.tr. It goes straight to the team, and you can attach a screenshot.
You can also send feedback from inside the app.

## Questions and suggestions

Open an issue in this repository. Issues are for questions about how SmileCat works
and for feature suggestions. For anything that needs device details or a screenshot,
email is the better channel.

## Privacy

Privacy policy: https://firstpoint.com.tr/apps/smilecat/privacy
Terms: https://firstpoint.com.tr/apps/smilecat/terms
SmileCat is not a medical device and does not replace your orthodontist's instructions. Follow the wear time your clinician prescribed.
